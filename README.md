# DomainScope for Claude

Research a company from its website. DomainScope is a catalog of company websites with the signals people ask about when they
research a business or a market: what a site is about, basic company signals, whether it publishes an AI catalog or MCP server, and how a
country's market is structured. This plugin connects Claude to the DomainScope server and adds four skills that tell Claude how to use it well.

## What you get

- **A connector** to the DomainScope server (`https://domainscope.scrapetheworld.org/mcp?profile=directory`) with 8 read-only tools: domain
  profile, company signals, AI catalog, MCP server search, and country-level market structure (market index, dimensions, concentration and
  country context).
- **Skills:** `company-research`, `market-lookup`, `ai-catalog-check` and `bigger-lists`.

## Try it

- "What is stripe.com, and what company signals does DomainScope have for it?"
- "Does shopify.com publish an AI catalog or an MCP server?"
- "How concentrated are the industries in Poland?"
- "Find domains that host MCP servers."

## Data handling

- No account and no sign-in. The connector is read-only: it never changes anything.
- The only thing sent is the domain, company name or search text you ask about, over HTTPS to the DomainScope server above. Nothing else
  is sent anywhere, and the plugin runs no code on your machine: it holds a connector entry and skill text only.
- Answers are read from stored records, not looked up live. Coverage varies by signal and by domain, so some fields can be empty.
  Category and industry labels are generated by AI and automated services; treat them as a strong starting point, not a verdict.
- The connector is not for personal data. It describes company websites.
